Technology in Football
Exploring Technology in Football
×

This project explores how technology has changed football and the ways athletes train and perform. Technologies such as GPS trackers, wearable sensors, video analysis, and artificial intelligence allow coaches and athletes to monitor performance, analyze gameplay, and make decisions about training. Research has shown that wearable technology can be used to measure and monitor physical activity and athletic performance (Li et al., 2016). Through Tracery, this project uses procedural text generation to demonstrate different ways technology can influence football players and teams.
